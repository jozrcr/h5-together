**A fix for "computers playing together": in 0.3.5 only one computer player per day really played.** H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. It is made first for the original game (3.1, the 2008 program, as Nival left it), and it still works with **MMH5.5** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## Fixed in 0.3.6

- **Computers playing together** (the host's choice, on by default since 0.3.5): only the first computer player of each day moved its heroes. The others did their town business (building, hiring, buying creatures) but their heroes stayed home from the second day on, so they took no mines and stayed weak. The game gives a player's heroes their daily movement back when that player's turn starts, and the add-on started the other computers' turns without that step. Now every computer player gets it, as when they play one after another. A robot game with four computer players: hero moves per computer over 5 days 36, 32, 36 and 33 (0.3.5: 33, 7, 10 and 9; one after another: 48, 22, 48 and 14).
- **Every PC needs 0.3.6** for computers to play together. With a 0.3.5 PC in the game, the computers play one after another (the game's own way), so a mixed game cannot go out of step.
- **The speed, measured again** with every computer playing: on a big map with four computer players, the computers' part of each day is about **3.5 times shorter** than with the game's own timings (61 s down to 18 s in a robot game). Playing together saves about 12% of it there (13% with two computer players on a smaller MMH5.5 map); the rest comes from the faster network timings, the shorter game steps and the faster pathfinding. (0.3.5's notes and README said "about 4 times": that figure counted computers that did nothing.)

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.6.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options.
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.6.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.6"** above the game's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.6 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.6."

## Check the download

- SHA-256 of `h5-together-0.3.6.zip`: `ZIP_SHA256`. GitHub built and tested the zip; then Valve's `steam_api.dll` was added on the maintainer's PC, byte for byte as in Steamworks SDK 1.65 (its SHA-256: `1cb8b7cb77923762d4e49b27e44295369a79e93b5d4282c5e840b268d28fe771`); the other files are as GitHub built them.

## Known issues

- **The original game cannot load maps made for MMH5.5** (with its editor or its random map generator), nor MMH5.5's saves: it crashes on their MMH5.5 objects. Use the game's own maps, or random maps made in the original game.
- **Start terrain of your faction** rebuilds the map at the start of a random map game: 20 to 30 s on a big map, on every PC.
- **A rare crash in the game's animation code** (`granny2.dll`), seen twice in one 3-player game with hero looks on. Not reproduced yet; unticking **"Others' looks"** (Options, H5 Together) on one PC turns the looks off for the whole game.
- **3-player games:** the game waits for the slowest PC at each step, so a slower PC still sets the pace of clicks for everyone.
- **A game saved with 0.3.5 and computers playing together** keeps the weak computers' small armies: the fix gives their heroes their moves back from the next day, not what they missed.
