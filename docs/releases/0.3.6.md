**A fix for weak computer players: with 0.3.5's default settings, only one computer player per day really played.** H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. It is made first for the original game (3.1, the 2008 program, as Nival left it), and it still works with **MMH5.5** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## Fixed in 0.3.6

- **Weak computer players.** 0.3.5 turned on "computers play together" by default (the host's choice), and it had a bug: only the first computer player of each day moved its heroes. The others did their town business (building, hiring, buying creatures) but their heroes stayed home from the second day on, so they took no mines and stayed weak. The game gives a player's heroes their daily movement back when that player's turn starts, and the add-on started the other computers' turns without that step. Now the computers play one after another again, as in the original game, and every one moves its heroes every day.
- **"Computers play together" is no longer in the Options.** Even with the bug fixed, several computers thinking and moving at the same time is not something the game was made for: three long robot games of nine (15 days, four computers, a 320x320 map) crashed with it; one after another, all such games ran their 15 days. And it saved only 12-19% of the wait.
- **Install 0.3.6 on every PC.** (If one PC still has 0.3.5, the computers also play one after another, at full strength.)
- **The speed, measured again** with every computer playing: on a big map with four computer players, the computers' part of each day is about **3 times shorter** than with the game's own timings (61 s down to 20 s in a robot game), from the faster network timings, the shorter game steps and the faster pathfinding. 0.3.5's notes and README said "about 4 times": that figure counted computers that did nothing.

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.6.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options.
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.6.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.6"** above the game's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.6 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.6."

## Known issues

- **The original game cannot load maps made for MMH5.5** (with its editor or its random map generator), nor MMH5.5's saves: it crashes on their MMH5.5 objects. Use the game's own maps, or random maps made in the original game.
- **Start terrain of your faction** rebuilds the map at the start of a random map game: 20 to 30 s on a big map, on every PC.
- **A rare crash in the game's animation code** (`granny2.dll`), seen twice in one 3-player game with hero looks on. Not reproduced yet; unticking **"Others' looks"** (Options, H5 Together) on one PC turns the looks off for the whole game.
- **3-player games:** the game waits for the slowest PC at each step, so a slower PC still sets the pace of clicks for everyone.
- **A game saved with 0.3.5** keeps the weak computers' small armies: from the next day they play normally, but they do not get back what they missed.
