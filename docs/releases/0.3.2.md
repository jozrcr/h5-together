**A smoother map on big games, computer players side by side, Undo End Turn, and a way back after a crash.** Version 0.3.2 brings what our 3-player games over Steam asked for. H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East with MMH5.5**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## New in 0.3.2

**A smoother adventure map** (each player's own screen):

- **Bigger model memory** (on). The game keeps the 3D models of the map in a shared block of graphics memory, made for its own zoom. Zoomed far out on a big map it overflowed every other frame, and the game threw every model away and built it again. Now it has room (about 80 MB more video memory). The picture is exactly the same.
- **Lighter animations** (on; Options, H5 Together, "Animations allégées"). Only when the map drops below about 55 frames a second, far-away models animate on 1 frame in 2 to 4. Battles and towns are not affected.
- **MMH5.5's scripts at their own pace** (the host's choice, on). With the faster co-op steps of H5 Together, MMH5.5's main script loop ran four times as often as MMH5.5 intends. It now runs at its original pace again.

  Measured on a 6-player map at 2560x1440, zoomed out to 250% (robot):

  | | Frames a second |
  |---|---|
  | 0.3.1 | 33 to 36 |
  | 0.3.2 | about 70 |

**Co-op** (the host's choice, from the next game; every player needs 0.3.2, or they stay off):

- **Computers play together** ("Ordinateurs ensemble", off by default). The computer players take their turns at the same time instead of one after another: their part of each day about 40% shorter with 2 or 3 of them. They may decide a little differently than in the original game, so it is off by default. Multiplayer only.
- **Undo End Turn** ("Annuler la fin de tour", off by default). With simultaneous turns, a player who pressed End Turn too early gets a "Resume my turn" button under the game's "Please wait for the other players..." banner, as long as another human still plays.
- **Quick resume after a crash** ("Sauvegarde si un joueur part", on). When a player's game crashes or loses its connection, every PC first saves the game as "Reprise" with that player still in it. To play on together: Multiplayer, LAN, Create, Load "Reprise". The player who crashed gets the save sent over Steam by itself.

**Saves and display:**

- **Each game its own autosaves** (on). Autosaves are named after their game: "6P-epic-3v1v1v1 [9D92] - Sauvegarde auto 1". Before, every game shared "Sauvegarde auto 1, 2, 3", so a new game overwrote yesterday's.
- **Frame rate limit and vertical sync** (off; Options, H5 Together). At most 30 to 240 frames a second (the game draws up to 400 in its menus, which only heats the PC), and the vertical sync on or off.

## Changed

- **Game rules, "Skills of each class: Unlocked"** now means: a hero may also learn the skills of **the other classes of its own faction**, at chance 2 (like ToE's rare skills), never another faction's. Before, it added every skill any class can learn, so an Academy hero could be offered Gating. Every player needs 0.3.2 for the host's game rules: with an older version in the game, the rules stay off.

## Fixed

- **The Options page sometimes did not save** (with many settings on): the add-on ran out of room for its hooks into the game, and the save hook was the last one in. Now it has room to spare, and the log says so if it ever runs out.

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.2.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options (the same line as before).
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.2.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.2"** above MMH5.5's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.2 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.2."
- At the start of a multiplayer game, every PC's log has the same line `co-op features: this game (N PCs): on: ...`.

## Known issues

- **Quick resume** has been tested by our robot only: a real crash over Steam has not happened yet.
- **3-player games:** the game waits for the slowest PC at each step, so a slower PC still sets the pace of clicks for everyone.
- **Long games:** the frame rate goes down as heroes and towns add up, and each new day starts with a short freeze (the autosave).

## Good to know

- **Every player the same version:** the co-op features (scripts at their own pace, computers together, Undo End Turn) and the host's game rules turn on only when every PC has 0.3.2. With an older PC in the game they stay off, for everyone.
- Everything else is as in [0.3.1](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/releases/tag/v0.3.1): online play over Steam, settings inside the game, the installer, crash reports, and the rest.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then make a bug report.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
