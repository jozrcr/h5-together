**Smoother co-op games, and hero looks that go to the right hero.** Version 0.3.1 fixes the two main problems found in our first 3-player games over Steam with 0.3.0. H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East with MMH5.5**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## Fixed in 0.3.1

- **Clicks and computer turns no longer lag in co-op games.** In 0.3.0, a click on the adventure map took about half a second over the internet, and the computer players moved in slow steps. There were three causes:
  - **A PC that fell behind stayed behind.** The game slows down a PC that runs ahead, but never speeds up one that is behind. Every other PC then waited for it, at every step. Now a PC that is behind runs up to 50% faster until it has caught up. The world still moves step by step, the same on every PC; only that PC's pace changes for a moment.
  - **During computer turns, the host's connection was full.** The computer players' big moves queued everything else behind them, sometimes for 2 seconds. The game's own connection now sends five times more often.
  - **On Linux (Proton), the add-on's Steam code could hold up the game's network calls** for 20 to 30 ms every 2 seconds. Fixed.

  Measured in robot games between a Windows PC and a Linux laptop over Steam (the laptop on battery, a slow PC on purpose):

  | | 0.3.0 | 0.3.1 |
  |---|---|---|
  | A click on the map, from sending to the move | 0.52 s | 0.12 s |
  | Time between two moves of the computer players (median) | 0.57 s | 0.15 s |
  | Host: average wait at each step | 0.58 s | 0.12 s |
  | Laptop: average wait at each step | 0.56 s | 0.21 s |

  The PCs never went out of step. LAN and GameRanger games use the same game connection, so they get the first two fixes too.

- **Hero looks go to the right hero.** On the hero screen, the **Look** button changed the hero selected on the map, not the hero the screen shows. So after the hero screen's arrows (or a hero opened from a town), you looked at hero B, clicked Look, and hero A changed. Now it is always the hero on the screen. In multiplayer, the change now also shows on the hero screen when the hero has no figure on the map (in a town, for example), and a look changed in the first seconds of a game counts at the same moment on every PC.

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.1.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options (the same line as before).
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.1.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.1"** above MMH5.5's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.1 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.1."

## Known issues

- **No "Invite friends..." button in the waiting room of a loaded game.** Your friends still see the game in **Multiplayer, LAN** and join from there.
- **Big maps with MMH5.5's scripts on** (6 players, huge maps) can make the adventure map slow on some PCs, more so with the camera zoomed far out. Battles are not affected.
- **During computer turns on big maps, the host's picture can stutter**: the computer players think between two pictures. It does not slow the turns down.

## Good to know

- **The connection sends more often:** up to about 140 KB per second while the computer players move, and a small packet every 10 ms the rest of the time.
- **The old behaviour**, if you ever need it: in `h5-together.ini`, `[net]` section, `catch_up_behind=0`, `keepalive_ms=50` and `send_interval_ms=10` (every player the same).
- Everything else is as in [0.3.0](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/releases/tag/v0.3.0): online play over Steam, settings inside the game, the installer, crash reports, and the rest.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then make a bug report.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
