**open-homm5 is now H5 Together.** Version 0.3.0 is the first release under the new name, and the first made for everyone, not only our group. H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East with MMH5.5**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, **online play with your Steam friends**, and a few extras. It is one file, `dinput8.dll`, plus a settings file, 24 ready random map templates and Valve's `steam_api.dll` for online play; the game's own files are never changed. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## Online play over Steam (new, a test)

Play over the internet with your **Steam friends**, without GameRanger or a VPN. The add-on carries the game's own LAN play through Steam's network, so your friends' games show in the game's LAN list as if you were in the same house.

1. **Every player:** main menu, **Options, H5 Together, Online play: Steam**, then restart the game. Steam must be running, and the game must be the Steam version.
2. **The host:** **Multiplayer, LAN, Create**, as usual. In the waiting room, **Invite friends...** opens Steam's invite window (or the add-on's own list of your Steam friends).
3. **The friends:** accept the invite in Steam. The game starts if it is not running, opens the LAN screen and joins the host's game by itself. Or open **Multiplayer, LAN** and join the host's game in the list.

- Everyone needs 0.3.0 with Online play on Steam; the host's Steam friends are the ones who see the game.
- While it is on, the LAN list shows only games through Steam. For GameRanger or a real LAN game, set Online play back to **Off**.
- Steam goes straight between the PCs when it can, or through Valve's relay servers (on a phone's 4G connection, for example). Every message is sent again until it arrives, as on a real LAN.
- The LAN screen shows a few lines about Steam (is it on, which friends play with H5 Together, why a game cannot be joined).
- **Tested** with two PCs, Windows and Linux (Proton), on home Wi-Fi and over 4G: about 4,000 world checks compared, all equal. **Not yet** with three or more players. Not for GOG copies.
- The zip carries Valve's `steam_api.dll` (from the Steamworks SDK), which the add-on loads only when online play is on. It is Valve's file, not covered by our licence (see `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`).

## The new name

The add-on was called **open-homm5** until 0.2.7. Install 0.3.0 over it as usual: the installer gives your files their new names (`open-homm5.ini` becomes `h5-together.ini`, the folder `open-homm5` becomes `h5-together`, and so on) and says so in one line. Your settings, hero looks and French packs are kept. The GitHub page moved to `jozrcr/h5-together`; the old links still lead there.

## New in 0.3.0

- **Settings inside the game.** Main menu, **Options**, **H5 Together** (or press **F3**, in the main menu or on the map). It is a page of the game's own Options screen, with the game's own check boxes, lists and sliders. The settings a player cares about sit on two pages side by side, **On this PC** and **Co-op games**. Click **Apply**; **Default** puts them back. A name that ends in `*` applies after a restart, `**` from the next game; the others apply at once. During a multiplayer game the host's and the advanced settings are read only. The settings are still saved in `h5-together.ini`.
- **A friendlier installer.** Before it copies anything, `install.bat` / `install.sh` show **your game**: the folder, Steam or GOG, the game's language, which MMH5.5 you have (RC19d, RC19k, or "a version we have not tested") and what each exe is. It also finds GOG copies and the folder that MMH5.5's own installer remembers, opens a folder window when it cannot find the game (type **B**), asks you to close the game when it is running, and warns in plain words when MMH5.5 is missing. It is still a zip with two scripts: there is no `setup.exe`.
- **The waiting room shows versions.** A line at the bottom says whether every player has the same H5 Together, and who has another version or none. It appears when the players have 0.3.0; a PC with 0.2.7 shows up as "older" or "none".
- **Crash reports.** If the game crashes, the add-on writes `h5-together-crash-<date>-<time>.txt` (what crashed, and where) and a small `.dmp` next to the log. They stay on your PC. The next start's log says so.
- **A "make a bug report" script.** `bug-report.bat` (or `bash bug-report.sh`) makes one zip on your Desktop with the log, your settings, the newest crash report and a list of versions. It sends nothing; you attach it to a [new issue](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/issues/new/choose) (the form asks for it). No saves, no maps.
- **A "new version" notice, only if you want it.** The first time the game starts, a small box asks "Check for new versions of H5 Together once a day?". If you say Yes, the main menu tells you when a newer version is out. It asks GitHub for the number of the latest version, at most once a day, and nothing else. Off by default; nothing is ever downloaded by the add-on.
- **Works next to another `dinput8.dll` mod.** Rename the other mod's file `dinput8_next.dll` before installing: H5 Together loads it and passes DirectInput on to it. (Tried with a stand-in mod; tell us how it goes with a real one.)
- **The add-on's words are in files.** Every panel's text now comes from one text file per language, in `bin\h5-together\texts\`. English and French come with it, and it follows the game's language. **Translators are welcome**: copy `en.txt`, translate the right-hand sides, and send it to us ([guide](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/src/mods/texts/TRANSLATING.md)). The reasons that panels give ("why this rule is not used") are now short and translated, and the "your turn" pop-up speaks French in a French game.
- **A picture and a tune for the main menu** (off by default). In Options, H5 Together, **Main menu picture**: As installed, Tribes of the East, Heroes V, or a random one at each start, each with its own music. Nothing changes unless you choose.
- **The main menu shows your version**, "H5 Together 0.3.0", above MMH5.5's version line.
- **The game's cursor stays on top.** The add-on's panels and buttons no longer cover the mouse cursor, and their buttons light up under the cursor like the game's own.
- **Small things:** the log is trimmed when it passes 5 MB (the old one is kept as `h5-together.old.log`); `dinput8.dll` now has a name, a version and a description in its file properties; the zip carries `LICENSE.txt` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`; GitHub forms for bug reports, feature ideas and translations.

## Also in this version (from 0.2.0 to 0.2.7)

- **Co-op:** quicker AI turns and clicks, less AI thinking on big maps, no long autosave freeze, LAN games over ZeroTier and other virtual networks, a fix for a rare host freeze, and a freeze detector.
- **Panels:** the player list on the map (F9), a "your turn" notification, other looks for your heroes (F7), and the host's game rules menu (F2): MMH5.5's switches chosen for each game, Tribes of the East start bonuses, game modes, MMH5.5's scripts on random maps, and unlocked class skills.
- **Random maps:** the in-game template builder (F11), 24 ready templates, teams, and start terrain that matches each faction (off by default).
- **Options, off by default:** farther camera zoom, black map edges, a quieter volume range, the original Heroes V music, French texts for the whole game. On Linux, the mouse wheel zooms out again.

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.0.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install:
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options.
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install this version.**

A one-click setup may come later.

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.0"** above MMH5.5's version line, and Options has an **H5 Together** page.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.0 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.0."

## Known issues

Found in our first games over Steam (3 players, Windows and Linux), to be fixed in 0.3.1:

- **Clicks on the adventure map can take about 0.2 s** in a game over the internet: every PC waits for the one that runs behind (in our games, a PC under Linux/Proton, even when its frame rate was high). A catch-up for a PC that falls behind is the next fix. Meanwhile, **Move display delay** at 150 ms (Options, H5 Together, Advanced; every player the same) makes moves smoother.
- **No "Invite friends..." button in the waiting room of a loaded game.** Your friends still see the game in **Multiplayer, LAN** and join from there.
- **Hero looks changed during a multiplayer game** may not show on the map for the other players.
- **Big maps with MMH5.5's scripts on** (6 players, huge maps) can make the adventure map slow on some PCs, more so with the camera zoomed far out. Battles are not affected.

## Good to know

- **Supported:** Steam Tribes of the East 3.1 with MMH5.5 RC19d or RC19k, started with the co-op exe `MMH55_Utility_64.exe` (also when renamed `H5_Game.exe` for GameRanger); Windows 10 and 11; Linux with Steam and Proton. **Not recognised:** MMH5.5's single-player exe `MMH55_64.exe` and Steam's 2025 `H5_Game.exe` (the add-on stays off and the game runs as it always did). **Not tried:** GOG, Steam Deck, Wine/Lutris/Heroic, the H5 Lobby app. **Not yet:** plain Tribes of the East without MMH5.5.
- **The settings page** was tested on Windows. It was not tried on Linux yet, nor with the greyed rows inside a real multiplayer game.
- **Everyone should have 0.3.0.** The host's game rules, the shorter co-op steps and others' hero looks need every player to have the same version, and the version line in the waiting room needs 0.3.0 on the PCs. A game that mixes 0.2.7 and 0.3.0 PCs was not tried as a whole.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then make a bug report.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
- **MMH5.5's own bugs** go to the MMH5.5 team.
