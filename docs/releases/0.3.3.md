**Follow your friends while you wait, and watch their battles live.** H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East with MMH5.5**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## New in 0.3.3

**Follow a friend's view** (each player's choice, on):

- While you wait for your turn, **click an ally's name in the player list**: your camera follows theirs on the adventure map, gliding, also down into the underground.
- Just over the game's turn banner, a button **"Stop following Pierre"**, with a line over it saying what Pierre is doing ("Pierre is in a battle", "in a town", "on the hero screen").
- It stops when you click the button or the name again, move the camera yourself, or your own turn begins.
- An **eye** after a name shows whom you follow. On their screen, an eye after your name shows that you follow them.
- **Allies only** (the same team). Each player still sees the map through their own fog: only the place where the camera looks is sent, a few times a second, so following cannot change the game.

**Watch their battle live** (each player's choice, on):

- While you follow an ally who fights, a second button shows beside the first: **"Watch the battle"**. It opens the game's own battle screen on their battle, live, also when the battle has already begun.
- **You only watch.** The battle gets none of your clicks or keys, and your PC sends nothing while you watch. The battle's buttons at the top right (auto combat, options, the white flag) show greyed out.
- Leave whenever you like with the small **"Stop watching"** button at the bottom right (or Esc), and come back as often as you like. At the end you see their results; **"Back to the map"** closes them.
- If your own battle starts meanwhile, watching stops at once.

**Settings** (Options, H5 Together): **"Allies may follow my view"** (off: your view stays yours, and your allies read that you do not share it) and **"Watch the followed ally's battles"** (off: no button). Both apply at once.

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.3.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options (the same line as before).
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.3.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.3"** above MMH5.5's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.3 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.3."
- In a game, a click on an ally's name writes `spectate: following ...'s view` in your log, and `spectate: ... follows this PC's view` in theirs.

## Known issues

- **Following and watching battles** have been tested by our robot only (two copies of the game on one PC, the game's world checks equal on both in every run). A real game over Steam, Esc while watching, and your own battle starting while you watch have not been tried yet.
- **3-player games:** the game waits for the slowest PC at each step, so a slower PC still sets the pace of clicks for everyone.
- **Long games:** the frame rate goes down as heroes and towns add up, and each new day starts with a short freeze (the autosave).

## Good to know

- **Mixing with 0.3.2:** the co-op features and the host's game rules work as in 0.3.2 when some PCs still have 0.3.2. Only following needs 0.3.3 on both PCs: a friend on 0.3.2 shows no view.
- Everything else is as in [0.3.2](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/releases/tag/v0.3.2): the smoother map, computers playing together, Undo End Turn, quick resume after a crash, online play over Steam, settings inside the game, the installer, crash reports, and the rest.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then make a bug report.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
