**open-homm5 0.2.5** lets you pick another look for your heroes, fixes the mouse wheel zoom-out on Linux, hides the duplicate maps that faction terrain created, and adds a quieter volume range and black map edges. It is one file, `dinput8.dll`, plus a settings file and 24 ready random map templates; the game's own files are never changed. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed** (RC19d or RC19k).

## New in 0.2.5

- **Hero looks.** On the hero screen, a small button in the top-right corner of "Armée & Stats" (or **F7**) opens a panel with the looks of the hero's own faction. Pick one and press **Appliquer**:
  - the hero's figure on the map changes at once;
  - battles use the new look;
  - the hero screen's 3D figure shows it the next time you open that screen.

  The portrait stays the hero's own. Your choices are saved per hero (`open-homm5-skins.ini`) and kept for every game. Computer players' heroes keep their normal look.
  - **In multiplayer** your friends see your looks too, but only if **every** player has this version of the add-on. Otherwise nobody's looks show in that game, so the PCs stay in sync.
  - Nothing changes until you pick a look. `[skins] skins=0` turns it off.
- **Mouse wheel on Linux (Wine / Proton).** The wheel could zoom in but never out: Wine reports the wheel differently from Windows. The add-on now fixes it automatically under Linux. Nothing changes on Windows.
- **No more duplicate maps.** With faction terrain, every rebuilt map appeared as another copy in the map lists. They are hidden now (kept on disk, because saved games need them).
- **Quieter sound** (`[audio] volume_curve=wide`, off by default). The sliders' lowest step becomes about 10 times quieter, and full volume stays the same.
- **Black map edges** (`[camera] edge_sky=none`, off by default). The violet sky that MMH5.5 put behind some random maps' edges becomes black, like unexplored land. Battle skies are unchanged.
- **Camera start distance** (`[camera] start_zoom=`, off by default) sets where the adventure camera starts.

## Also in this version (from 0.2.0 to 0.2.4)

- **In-game template builder** (Modèles... on the random map screen), recipe maps that friends rebuild by name, teams from the template.
- **Starting terrain matching each faction** (`[rmg] faction_terrain=1`, host).
- **Host freeze fix** and freeze detector; **MMH5.5 RC19k support**; farther camera zoom; French texts (`[lang] texts=fr`).
- Player list, "your turn" notification, faster multiplayer, no autosave freeze.

## Install

1. Download `open-homm5-0.2.5.zip` below and extract it.
2. Install:
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`.
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options.
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install this version.** It's needed for hero looks to show in multiplayer.

The installer keeps your `open-homm5.ini`. New settings work without being in it; the new file in the zip lists them all.

## Check that it works

- The first line of `open-homm5.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `open-homm5 hook DLL 0.2.5 loaded`.
- On the hero screen there is a small look button at the top right of "Armée & Stats".
- **On Linux,** after a few turns of the mouse wheel, the log says `wheel: Wine reports the mouse wheel as steps ... from now on the game gets positions`.

## Good to know

- **French texts:** the corrected French pack (lobby mode labels) is shared within the group, not in this zip.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then send `open-homm5.log`.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
