**Following and watching, polished after the first real games; stay and watch after a defeat; the computer bonus in loaded games.** H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East with MMH5.5**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. You need the game with **MMH5.5 already installed** (RC19d or RC19k). Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## New in 0.3.4

**Following a friend** (from the first evenings with three players over Steam):

- **Click the eye or the name** to start and stop following. Nothing in the middle of the map any more: right after the eye, a short line says where your friend is when not on the map ("dans une ville", "dans un menu"...), and while they fight a small **"Watch the battle"** button sits there.
- **Your camera goes to their hero at once**, also when they are in a town or a battle.
- **Watching works when they are already fighting**: 0.3.3 waited for a view that never came and said "does not share their view".
- **While watching:** a **right click** shows a creature's card, the mouse shows what lies under it, the wheel works. The left button and the keys still do nothing to their battle, and your PC still sends nothing.
- **Watching ends by itself** when their PC says they left that battle (when it was their game loss, the results never came and the watcher stayed in the finished battle).

**Stay and watch after a defeat** (each player's choice, on): when you lose the game (your last hero and no town) while your friends play on, closing the game's results no longer sends you back to the lobby. You stay on the map, play no more, and can follow your team and watch their battles. Esc, Quit leaves. Off: back to the lobby as in the original game.

**"Resume my turn"** is now a round button over the game's hourglass, its name at the left of the wheel for a few seconds and under the mouse.

**The computer bonus in a loaded game:** the host's F2 "Computer bonus" (Off, 40% ... 140%) now also applies when you load a save, from the next week: every PC sets it at the same moment of the game. Load a save from the 7th day of a week with the bonus Off, and the computers get no extra gold and creatures that week. What they already got stays.

**Also:**

- **Black map edges in the underground** too (`[camera] edge_sky=none`).
- **Quick resume:** the "Reprise" save now reaches the friend who crashed whole. With a big save, the host offered it while the game was still writing it, the friend got a cut-off file, and his seat in the loaded game was locked.
- **Quick resume** makes no "Reprise" save for a player who had already lost the game (it used to replace the last useful one).

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.4.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options (the same line as before).
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.4.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.4"** above MMH5.5's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.4 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.4."
- In a multiplayer game, the host's log says `the host's computer bonus: H55_AICheatMode = ... set in the game on every PC`.

## Check the download

- SHA-256 of `h5-together-0.3.4.zip`: `ZIP_SHA256`. GitHub built and tested the zip; then Valve's `steam_api.dll` was added on the maintainer's PC, byte for byte as in Steamworks SDK 1.65 (its SHA-256: `1cb8b7cb77923762d4e49b27e44295369a79e93b5d4282c5e840b268d28fe771`); the other files are as GitHub built them.
- The zip is built and tested by GitHub from the code in this repository. Nothing in it changes the game's own files. [Is it safe?](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together#is-it-safe)

## Known issues

- **A rare crash in the game's animation code** (`granny2.dll`) hit two PCs of the same 3-player game, once in a battle and once in a town, with hero looks on. Not reproduced yet; until then, unticking **"Others' looks"** (Options, H5 Together) on one PC turns the looks off for the whole game, and that game was stable.
- **3-player games:** the game waits for the slowest PC at each step, so a slower PC still sets the pace of clicks for everyone.
- **The army's placement before a battle** is not shown to a watcher until the battle starts: the game sends it only then.

## Good to know

- **Mixing with 0.3.3:** the co-op features and the host's rules work as before when some PCs still have 0.3.3; the computer bonus in a loaded game needs 0.3.4 on every PC.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then make a bug report.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
