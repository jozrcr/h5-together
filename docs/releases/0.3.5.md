**The original game without MMH5.5; a Linux friend can join a big random map; watching a battle shows the army's placement; hero details, a sword in the player list, the skill wheel; new co-op defaults.** H5 Together is an add-on for **Heroes of Might and Magic V: Tribes of the East**: faster, steadier co-op games against the computer, online play with your Steam friends, and a few extras. **From this version on it is made first for the original game** (3.1, the 2008 program, as Nival left it), and it still works with **MMH5.5** (RC19d or RC19k) for groups that play with it. Windows, or Linux with Proton.

## New in 0.3.5

**The original game, without MMH5.5:**

- H5 Together now works with the original Tribes of the East 3.1, the 2008 program, also from a game folder where MMH5.5 is installed: H5 Together hides MMH5.5's files from it, so you get the original game, and MMH5.5 still starts as before from its own exe. Nothing on disk is changed or moved.
- **The installer can make `bin\H5_Original_4GB.exe`**, the original game that may use 4 GB of memory (the original exe has only 2 GB, and a big map at a high resolution runs out of it). With your yes, it fetches only the 2008 exe (about 5.4 MB) from MMH5.5's official release on GitHub and checks it is exactly that file; without internet it takes it from MMH5.5's installer file. Start it with a double-click, Steam running. On Linux the installer prints the Steam launch option for it.
- **Its own profile and saves** (`Documents\H5 Together\Original game`): a save of one game crashes the other. Your profile's settings are copied there at the first start, never the saves.
- **Endless simultaneous turns** for the original game (the host's choice): simultaneous turns last the whole game, as MMH5.5's PEST does.
- The game rules menu (F2) is not there: its rules are all MMH5.5's. Over Steam you see only games of your own kind (original game or MMH5.5).

**Online and co-op:**

- **A Linux friend can join a Windows host's big random map.** Under Proton, after building a big map (25 to 30 s), the joining PC often failed with "timeout 0.18.0". The cause was ours: the add-on dropped network messages that the game read more than 3 s after they arrived, and that could be the host's single "you are in" answer. Now nothing is dropped for its age, as on a real network. Joining PCs also get up to 300 s for their join (`[net] join_timeout_s`).
- **Watching a friend's battle now shows their army's placement** before the battle, as they move their stacks. Before, a watcher saw the automatic placement until the battle began. Only what you see changes, never the game: no risk of the PCs going out of step. A merge or a split of stacks shows when the battle starts.
- **A sword on an ally's row** in the player list while they fight. Click it to follow them and open their battle at once.
- **Hero details in the waiting room:** hover a starting hero's picture (or hold the right button on it): besides its name and specialty, the tooltip gives its class, its starting skills and what its specialty does.
- **Skill wheel** (the original game): a small wheel button on the hero screen, next to the Look button, shows what the hero's class can learn: each skill's chance, every ability with what it needs first, and what the hero already has (gold) or can take now (light frame). The arrows show the other classes.
- **Builder maps:** the team arrows move you to an open start of your own team, so teammates can pick their places; teams stay the template's.

**New defaults** (the group's usual co-op setup; every one can still be turned off in Options, H5 Together):

- Endless simultaneous turns, computers playing together, Undo End Turn and start terrain of your faction (the host's choices).
- The wider volume range, online play over Steam (without Steam the game keeps its own LAN play), a limit of 180 frames a second.
- Your own settings file keeps what you set; only the lines you never changed follow the new defaults.

**Also:**

- The installer's last screen names the exe to start: `H5_Original_4GB.exe` first when it made it, MMH5.5's exe as the other choice.
- If one of H5 Together's panels hits an error, it now comes back after 5 s instead of staying off until the game restarts, and the log says where the error was.

## Install

1. Download `h5-together-0.3.5.zip` below and extract the whole zip (Windows: right-click it, **Extract All**).
2. Install it over your old version (your settings and hero looks are kept):
   - **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. Windows may show a warning box ("Windows protected your PC"): that is normal for a new, unsigned file; see [what to click](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together/blob/main/docs/install.md#windows-protected-your-pc-and-antivirus).
   - **Linux (Steam / Proton):** run `bash install.sh` and paste the line it prints into the game's Steam launch options.
3. **Everyone in the same co-op game should install 0.3.5.**

## Check that it works

- The main menu shows **"H5 Together 0.3.5"** above the game's version line.
- The first line of `h5-together.log` (in the game's `bin` folder) says `H5 Together hook DLL 0.3.5 loaded`.
- In a multiplayer waiting room, the bottom line says "Every player has H5 Together 0.3.5."

## Check the download

- SHA-256 of `h5-together-0.3.5.zip`: `ZIP_SHA256`. GitHub built and tested the zip; then Valve's `steam_api.dll` was added on the maintainer's PC, byte for byte as in Steamworks SDK 1.65 (its SHA-256: `1cb8b7cb77923762d4e49b27e44295369a79e93b5d4282c5e840b268d28fe771`); the other files are as GitHub built them.
- The zip is built and tested by GitHub from the code in this repository. Nothing in it changes the game's own files. [Is it safe?](https://github.com/jozrcr/h5-together#is-it-safe)

## Known issues

- **The original game cannot load maps made for MMH5.5** (with its editor or its random map generator), nor MMH5.5's saves: it crashes on their MMH5.5 objects. Use the game's own maps, or random maps made in the original game.
- **Start terrain of your faction** rebuilds the map at the start of a random map game: 20 to 30 s on a big map, on every PC.
- **A rare crash in the game's animation code** (`granny2.dll`), seen twice in one 3-player game with hero looks on. Not reproduced yet; unticking **"Others' looks"** (Options, H5 Together) on one PC turns the looks off for the whole game.
- **3-player games:** the game waits for the slowest PC at each step, so a slower PC still sets the pace of clicks for everyone.

## Good to know

- **Mixing with 0.3.4:** endless simultaneous turns, the army placement for watchers and the sword need 0.3.5 on every PC; the rest works as before when some PCs still have 0.3.4. A Linux PC that joins needs 0.3.5 itself.
- **If a game freezes,** wait 30 seconds before closing it, then make a bug report.
- **Uninstall:** `uninstall.bat` (Windows) or `bash uninstall.sh` (Linux).
